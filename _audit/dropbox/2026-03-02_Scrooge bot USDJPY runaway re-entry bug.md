### The Core Problems

**1. Re-entry loop with no loss memory**
The bot hit its stop, then re-entered the *exact same trade* 10 minutes later — 19 more times. There is zero trade outcome awareness. It has no concept of "I just lost this trade."

**2. No max consecutive loss / daily loss limit**
A properly configured bot should have halted after 3–5 consecutive stops at minimum. This ran unchecked for 3 hours.

**3. Signal never invalidated**
The pitchfork signal kept firing despite price being *above* entry (stops were being hit at 111.49, meaning price moved ~98 pips against the trade). The signal logic has no confirmation that the setup is still valid after a loss.

**4. Stops hit in ~1 second**
Every trade filled and stopped out in 1 second. Price was already at or above 111.49 at entry time. The bot was entering into an already-invalidated zone repeatedly.

---

### Estimated Damage

At 1000 units USD/JPY, 98 pip loss each:
- ~$8.87 USD per trade (at ~110.51 rate)
- **×20 trades = ~$177 USD** (plus spread/commission × 20)

Small in dollar terms here, but catastrophic if scaled up.

### Immediate Fixes Needed

**In your bot logic:**

```python
# 1. Daily loss limit
```
