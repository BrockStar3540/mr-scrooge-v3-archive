## The Three Systems and Their Sequencing

### 1. BOT (main.py) — Signal + Entry + Management loop (every 5 min)
- **Entry**: `place_entry_order()` → **always MARKET order** (FOK). Comment in code: "OANDA cancels limit orders placed at/near current price (WOULD_TRIGGER_IMMEDIATELY)."
- **SL/TP**: Set atomically via `stopLossOnFill` / `takeProfitOnFill` on the market order
- **Management** (every 5 min): TradeManager.manage_position() → ratchets SL only, never places market orders
- **Green exit**: hourly check, closes via limit-first (falling back to market)

### 2. RATCHET (trade_manager.py) — SL advancement (every 5 min, inside bot loop)
- **Stage 1**: SL → breakeven when price is 50% of way to TP1
- **Stage 2**: SL → TP1 price when price passes TP1
- **Stage 3**: Hybrid trailing stop = max(hourly candle trail, 80% profit lock, 1.5x ATR trail)
- Only output: `move_stop` action → bot calls `router.move_stop()` → OANDA `modify_trade_stop`

### 3. HARVESTER (harvester/main.py) — Separate daemon, polls every 30 sec
- **T1** (2% equity): close 25%, SL → breakeven
- **T2** (4% equity): close 25%, SL → entry + T1 pips
- **T3** (6% equity): close 25%, SL → entry + T2 pips
- **Runner**: remaining 25% rides to OANDA native TP2
- Uses **limit-first** close (opposing limit order, 90s expiry, market fallback after 3 polls)

### The Conflict Points

**Problem 1: Bot enters at MARKET, harvester exits at LIMIT — asymmetric friction**
```
ENTRY:  Market order → pays full spread (taker)
EXIT:   Harvester limit → gets maker fill (good)
BUT:    SL exits are OANDA native → market fill (taker again)
```
You're paying taker spread on entry AND on every SL exit. Only the harvester partials get limit fills. On the AUD/CAD trade, the entry spread was $5.20 and the SL exit spread was $3.90 — **$9.10 in taker friction** vs $1.25 on the harvester's limit partial.

**Problem 2: Ratchet and Harvester both move SL — race condition**
The ratchet (5-min loop) and harvester (30-sec loop) both modify the stop loss:
- Ratchet Stage 1: moves SL to breakeven at 50% of TP1
- Harvester T1: moves SL to breakeven at 2% equity threshold

These are **different triggers for the same action**. Whichever fires first wins. But then:
- Ratchet Stage 2: moves SL to TP1 price
- Harvester T2: moves SL to entry + T1 pips

These could be **different prices**. The ratchet uses the TP1 level. The harvester uses entry + a calculated pip offset based on equity %. They could fight.

**Problem 3: Harvester syncs quantity but bot doesn't know tier state**
```python
# main.py line 820:
# Sync remaining_quantity from OANDA (harvester may have closed partials)
```
The bot syncs the *units* from OANDA, but it uses `partial_1_taken` as a sentinel for "TP1 passed" (Stage 2 ratchet). The harvester sets its own `tiers_hit` in its own state file.
