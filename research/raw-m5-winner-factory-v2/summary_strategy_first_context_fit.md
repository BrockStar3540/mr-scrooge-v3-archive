## Interpretation

- Do not start with regimes. Start with the strategy and pair.

- The broad parent strategy can lose; a regime is promoted when a post-hoc context repeatedly turns that parent into a survivor.

- The existing `h1_regime`, `m15_trend`, and session labels are candidate descriptors, not authority.

- If repeated context descriptors are too crude, the next step is to define a new regime from the shared OHLCV conditions of those surviving strategy/pair cells.
